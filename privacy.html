Privacy Policy

Siddharth & Nandita Wedding RSVP · Last updated 29 October 2026

This website is a private RSVP page for the wedding of Siddharth and Nandita. This policy explains what information we collect when you RSVP and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you sign in and submit an RSVP, we collect:

  • Your name and email address, provided through Google Sign-In;
  • Your phone number;
  • Whether you will attend, and the number of guests in your party;
  • Any optional message you choose to leave for the couple.

We use Google Sign-In only to confirm your email address so that each guest has a single RSVP they can return to and edit. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access any other information from your Google account.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to plan and manage this wedding — for example, to count guests, arrange seating and catering, and contact you with details about the event. It is not used for advertising or any unrelated purpose.

How your information is stored and shared

Responses are stored privately in a Google Sheet accessible only to the couple and the people helping organise the wedding. We do not sell your information or share it with any third party, except the services that run this site (Google, which provides sign-in and spreadsheet storage).

Your choices

You can update your RSVP at any time by signing in again. If you would like your information corrected or deleted, please contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

Contact
